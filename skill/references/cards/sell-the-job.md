## Spot it
The founder repeatedly does work for customers beyond the product (books, reconciliation,
marketing lists, tax fixes) and treats it as a favour.

## Proof it is installed
The user frames this work as an offer or a bundle piece without a nudge.

## Why it matters
Customers hire you to get a job done. The jobs you already do by hand are the bundle
they will pay for.
