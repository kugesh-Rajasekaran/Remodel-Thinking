## Spot it
Long runs of one-letter answers ("a", "A", "a") to options the agent framed, often late at
night, while a design is locked in.

## Proof it is installed
The user states what they are optimising for before choosing, without a nudge.

## Why it matters
Whoever frames the options makes most of the decision. A founder needs to set the frame.
