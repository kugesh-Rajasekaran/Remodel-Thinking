## Spot it
A feature or integration is researched, designed and built, and cost or price comes up
only afterwards, or it is dropped because of cost.

## Proof it is installed
Build requests start with the customer and the price ("three customers asked for X, they'd pay
Y, it costs us Z"), without a nudge.

## Why it matters
Price is the fastest test of value. Two hours of asking can save weeks of building.
